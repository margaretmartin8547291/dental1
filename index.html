What happens at your first visit

Your visit, step by step

We aim to make your visit as calm and predictable as possible, especially if you feel nervous about seeing a dentist.

1

Simple booking

Send a request through this page or call us. We agree a time and confirm details with you.

2

Check-up and X‑rays

We examine your teeth and gums, and where appropriate, take X‑rays to understand what is happening.

3

Treatment options

We explain any issues we find, available treatment options, expected timelines, and estimated costs.

4

Ongoing care

You decide which treatments to proceed with. We arrange future appointments that work with your schedule.

We always provide information about benefits, risks, and alternatives. You can ask questions at any time and you are free to decline treatment.

Frequently asked questions

Do you guarantee results?

No. While we aim for the best possible outcomes, treatment results vary between patients. We explain expected results clearly before you decide.

Do you treat nervous patients?

Yes. We allow extra time where needed, explain each step, and you can pause or stop the appointment at any time.

How do you use my information?

We use your information to manage your dental care, handle appointments, and meet legal and regulatory obligations. Details are in our Privacy Policy below.

Is this page an emergency service?

No. If you have a serious injury, uncontrolled bleeding, or severe swelling, contact emergency services or urgent dental care in your area immediately.
